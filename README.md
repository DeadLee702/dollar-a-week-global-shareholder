🌍 The 10-Cent Initiative

A Global Initiative

The 10-Cent Initiative begins with an idea simple enough for anyone, anywhere to understand:

Participate at a level you can afford.

The starting point is the equivalent of 10 U.S. cents in your local currency.

It is not a required weekly payment. It is not a subscription. It is not a debt.

It is an invitation to participate.

A person may contribute 10 cents, more than 10 cents, or less when circumstances require it.

The important thing is participation.

---

🌎 Everyone, Everywhere

This initiative is designed for people everywhere.

It does not matter:

- What country you live in
- What language you speak
- What culture you come from
- Whether you live in a major city, a small town, or a remote village
- Whether you can contribute 10 cents, 25 cents, $1, $10, or more

The initiative begins with a simple amount because simple ideas are easy to understand.

The equivalent of 10 cents can mean something different in different parts of the world.

The app therefore presents contributions in the participant's local currency and language.

---

📍 Start Where You Are

The application begins by establishing where the participant is.

With permission, the app uses live location information to identify the participant's geographic area as accurately as available data allows.

The purpose is not to assume who someone is.

The purpose is to understand where they are.

The app can then present the languages commonly used in that area.

The participant chooses their language.

If their language or local dialect is not listed, they can enter it themselves.

The person chooses the language.

---

🗣️ Speak to People in Terms They Understand

Translation alone is not enough.

The initiative is global, which means information must be understandable to people with different languages, cultures, currencies, and everyday experiences.

The application is designed to present the same underlying information in terms people can understand.

The facts remain the facts.

The way those facts are communicated can adapt to the person.

---

💰 The Power of 10 Cents

The starting point is intentionally small.

Imagine 2.5 billion people participating at the equivalent of 10 cents each:

$250 million per week.

Now imagine participation growing.

At the same 2.5 billion participants:

$0.10 → $250 million/week

$1 → $2.5 billion/week

$10 → $25 billion/week

The larger the participation and the larger the voluntary contribution, the greater the collective capital becomes.

The initiative does not need to begin at its eventual scale.

It needs to begin.

---

The purpose of the collective capital is to build, acquire, improve, and invest in productive assets and infrastructure around the world.

The allocation can include:

- Property acquisition

💧 Water

- Storage
- Distribution
- Water infrastructure

🏠 Housing

- Affordable housing
- Community housing
- New construction

⚡ Energy

- Solar

- Energy storage
- Local energy systems
- Energy infrastructure

🏗️ Productive Infrastructure

- Agriculture
- Manufacturing
- Transportation
- Communications
- Schools

📈 Investments

Collective capital can also be allocated to investments.

This can include:

- Real estate
- Businesses
- Financial assets
- Digital assets
- Cryptocurrency
- Other investment opportunities permitted by the initiative's rules

The objective is not simply to collect money.

The objective is to turn collective participation into collective productive capacity.

---

🤖 Deterministic Investment Allocation

Investment decisions are not intended to depend on one person's opinion.

A deterministic AI system can operate within defined rules for investment allocation.

The system evaluates available opportunities according to established parameters and produces decisions according to those rules.

The process is designed to be:

- Repeatable
- Auditable
- Rule-based
- Transparent
- Deterministic

The AI is not the authority.

The protocol is the authority.

The application records the decisions and makes the relevant information visible.

---

🔎 Follow the Money

Transparency is fundamental to the initiative.

Participants should not have to wonder:

Where did the money go?

The application is designed to show it.

Participants can see:

- Contributions
- Allocations
- Acquisitions
- Investments
- Infrastructure
- Assets
- Projects
- Transactions
- Returns
- Distributions
- Important decisions
- Supporting records

Transparency should not require someone to understand complicated financial charts.

The application presents information in understandable language first, with deeper information available for anyone who wants to examine it.

See the money.

See where it went.

See what it produced.

---

🏗️ Building the Asset Base

The initiative is designed to build a broad collective asset base.

The progression can include:

LAND → WATER → ENERGY → HOUSING → PRODUCTIVE INFRASTRUCTURE → INVESTMENTS

Existing infrastructure can be acquired, improved, expanded, or supported.

Where infrastructure does not exist, the initiative can work toward creating it.

The objective is not limited to one type of asset or one geographic location.

The objective is productive capacity that serves people.

---

🌎 When the Asset Base Is Established

There is a point at which the initiative's focus can shift.

Once the necessary acquisitions and foundational infrastructure have been established globally, the collective assets and investments become an ongoing source of economic activity.

At that stage, the system can move increasingly from:

BUILDING THE ASSET BASE

toward:

GENERATING VALUE FROM THE ASSET BASE

The resulting economic value can be used according to the initiative's established allocation rules, including distributions to everyone participating.

The long-term cycle is:

PEOPLE → CONTRIBUTIONS → ASSETS → PRODUCTION & INVESTMENT → VALUE → PEOPLE

---

📊 $35 Billion a Week

The 10-Cent Initiative is designed with global scale in mind.

At 3.5 billion participating people, an average contribution of:

$10 per week = $35 billion per week.

That is:

$35,000,000,000 every week.

At that scale, the initiative can operate across multiple categories simultaneously.

For example, a future allocation model could direct:

$30 billion/week → infrastructure, acquisitions, housing, water, energy, land, and productive development

$5 billion/week → investments, including real estate, financial assets, digital assets, and cryptocurrency

The allocation is not limited to those numbers.

The system can evolve as the asset base, opportunities, and needs change.

The important point is the scale of collective economic capacity.

---

🔄 Contributions Can Grow Naturally

The initiative does not require everyone to contribute the same amount.

Someone may begin with the equivalent of 10 cents.

Someone else may contribute $1.

Another person may contribute $10.

Another may contribute considerably more.

Participation does not require someone to contribute beyond what they can reasonably afford.

The contribution can scale naturally as the participant chooses.

There is no need to pressure someone into increasing their contribution.

People can see what is happening and decide for themselves.

---

💳 Contributions Are Not a Subscription

A contribution is not automatically a recurring charge.

Someone can participate without signing up for a recurring payment they might later forget about.

A person can make a contribution, see how the system works, follow the money, and decide whether and when they want to contribute again.

The initiative is designed around voluntary participation.

---

🌐 One Human Initiative

The initiative is not limited to one country.

It is not limited to one language.

It is not limited to one culture.

It is not limited to people living in cities.

It is designed for people everywhere.

Different languages.

Different cultures.

Different places.

Different circumstances.

One human population.

The application is built to meet people where they are rather than requiring everyone to adapt to one country's way of doing things.
